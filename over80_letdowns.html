The flagged players

Each player is compared only with players at his position rated within three points of him, so an 88 receiver is measured against other high-80s receivers. SD below peers is how far under that group he sits. Plays like is the rating at the same percentile of the whole position as his production.

Why offense only. The same test on defense found nobody: no edge rusher rated 80+ was quiet enough to rule out two or three unlucky games. Box scores also can't see what most interior linemen and cornerbacks are paid to do.
  • Two or three games is not a season. A single bad game against a good defense can put a player here.
  • Injuries don't show in a box score. A player who got hurt early in a game looks like a quiet day.
  • Role matters. Penn State's two backs split carries, and receivers on run-first teams see fewer targets — that's why low team passing volume gets its own group below.
