Division I · Power Ratings

CFB Ranker — all of Division I, one formula

A Sagarin-style points model. FBS strength from EA College Football 27 rosters; FCS from recruiting, returning production & the transfer portal. Adjusted for strength of schedule (common opponents, 3 levels deep) and roster experience. Ratings read like a spread — the gap between two teams is the projected neutral-field margin. Game results then move each team: winning by more than expected raises a rating, and losses cost extra. Use the FBS / FCS filter, or click any column to sort.

How to read it. Rating is a Sagarin-like points number centered near 75 — the gap between two teams is the projected neutral-field margin. SOS is the points adjustment for schedule strength (+ tougher, − softer). Talent and Experience are 0–100 percentile scores across all teams (100 = best roster / most veteran), shown as bars. FCS teams have no EA roster or returning-production data, so those cells are blank.
NIL $M is 2026 priced roster value — the sum of each player's valuation, On3's deal-based methodology (via The NIL Standard; estimates, not audited) — display only, not part of the rating. Only the top ~25 spenders have published On3 values (others blank). The +% is spend vs. the team's full-conference average (lower-tier teams filled from a rescaled model), so a big green number flags a disproportionate spender — e.g. Texas Tech in the Big 12.
Squares mark tier: Power-conference / independent · Group of Five · FCS. Weights are adjustable and auto-calibrated.
