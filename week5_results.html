Correction: no bunching problem

An earlier version of this report said the model projects games “about half as lopsided” as they turn out, comparing its average predicted margin (9.3) with the average actual margin (17.7). That comparison was wrong: final scores always swing wider than predictions because games add noise on top. Measured properly — how actual margins scale with predicted ones — the model is fine: 1.25 this week against 1.33 for the market, and 1.07 over the season. Week 5 was simply a lopsided week for everyone. Alabama, Ohio State and Miami did win by far more than the model expected, but by more than the market expected too. See the season report for the full check.
