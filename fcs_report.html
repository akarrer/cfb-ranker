FCS ratings, rebuilt on the poll and Week 0 results

The first FCS audit failed hard (36% straight-up) because EA has no FCS rosters and CFBD's returning-production feed is FBS-only. Two fixes: anchor FCS talent to the 2026 Stats Perform FCS Top 25, and update every rating with the games actually played. Here's the result.

Fixed at the top, honest about the tail. Straight-up accuracy on the same 53 FCS games jumped from 36% to 57%. The Top 25 now reads like the real FCS — Montana State, South Dakota State, Illinois State — while the ~100 unranked teams remain provisional.

What changed

  • Poll anchor. The 2026 Stats Perform FCS Top 25 now sets talent for ranked teams (No. 1 highest → No. 25), overriding the noisy recruiting/portal synthesis that had floated Mercyhurst and Long Island to the top. (Note: AP doesn't poll FCS — this is the standard FCS media poll.)
  • Week 0 results update. A capped Elo-style nudge moves every team toward its actual outcome — so Youngstown State climbed after routing Mercyhurst 43–10, and South Dakota State rose to FCS No. 2.

Where it's still soft

57% is above coin-flip but well short of the FBS model's 75%. The reason: only the Top 25 are anchored — the other ~100 FCS teams still ride the thin recruiting/portal synthesis, and most FCS games involve at least one unranked team. Extending a rating source deeper (a full FCS results/SP+ rating) is the next lever.
