The first non-losing week for the 7+ disagreements: Northwestern +21.5, UCLA and James Madison cashed. Season: 18–30–1.

The honest read after two graded weeks: the model picks winners, not spreads. It is 184–55 straight up (77%) across Weeks 3 and 4, but 63–67–2 against the spread. The strongest evidence is line movement: between Sunday and kickoff, the line moved toward the model's pick 52 times and away from it 40, an average of just +0.2 points. A model with a real edge sees the market come to it consistently. This one doesn't, and its average miss on the final margin (12.2) was again worse than the market's (11.4). The tier rules built on Week 3 — close games good, mismatches bad — were reading noise.
