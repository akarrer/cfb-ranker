Read these as leads, not verdicts.
  • The samples are tiny. Excluding FCS games leaves most teams with one or two FBS games so far; everyone here has two or three. A player needs two games to qualify at all.
  • Box scores miss a lot. There is no snap count, coverage, or blocking data here, so offensive linemen, coverage corners, and blocking tight ends can't show up. Tackle totals partly reflect scheme and how often a defense is on the field.
  • EA is already moving. Its in-season update changed 1,255 ratings since August. Legree (70→79), Burgess (76→79), Stokes (73→76), Coenen (75→77) and Omosigho (77→79) were all bumped and still look underrated.
