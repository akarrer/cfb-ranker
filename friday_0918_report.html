Houston is the one to think about. It covered by 5.5 and lost rating anyway, because the losses-hurt-more setting (1.5× penalty on the loser) is bigger than the credit for playing a better team close on the road. That is the setting doing what you asked for, but it means a gutsy road loss moves a team down about as much as a lopsided one.

Oregon and Miami are the other lessons. The 28-point cap is working as intended on Oregon (no reward for running up the score on an FCS team), and Miami's jump to #15 is the model catching up to a team whose roster talent (97) has been well ahead of its rating all season.
