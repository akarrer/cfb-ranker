What it got right

  • Every clear game. All six favorites the model liked by a real margin held — USC, Virginia, Florida State, Stanford, Eastern Michigan, and North Dakota State all won.
  • No confident call was wrong. The two misses were the two closest predictions on the board (+0.7 and +3.0) — the model had them as coin-flips, not convictions.

Where it missed

  • North Carolina 15, TCU 10 (neutral) — model leaned TCU by 0.7, the definition of a toss-up. A pick'em that flipped.
  • Memphis 27, UNLV 21 — model had UNLV +3.0 at home; Memphis went in and won by six. A one-possession game inside the model's noise band.

Calibration: right direction, shy on margin

The model under-projected the margin in all six games it got right, by ~14 points on average (North Dakota State by 26, Virginia by 26). Two structural reasons: preseason SOS is deliberately muted (week 0 runs it at ×0.25), which compresses ratings toward the mean, and the points scale is conservative. It calls winners well but expects closer games than a weak-opponent opening slate delivers.
  • Fix in flight: as the schedule ramps to full weight by week 4, spread widens and margin error should fall.
  • Caveat: this slate was P4/FBS vs. FCS & G5 — easy winners. The real test is competitive matchups in the coming weeks.
