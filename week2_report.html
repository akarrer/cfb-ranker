The lesson I gave you last week was wrong

Big disagreements with Vegas lost. Small ones won.

After SMU–Florida State I suggested only treating model-vs-market gaps of a touchdown or more as signal. This week says the opposite: FBS games where the model disagreed with the closing line by 7+ points went ; games within 7 went . Large gaps are where the model is missing something the market knows. Sample sizes are small, so read this as "big edges are not reliable," not as a proven reverse strategy.
