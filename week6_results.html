What the week says

The model's worst week of the season by a distance. Part of it was a genuinely wild Saturday; most of it wasn't.

A wild Saturday

38/57

Market favorites won 38 of 57. Georgia won at Alabama, Oklahoma beat Texas, South Carolina won by 19 at Florida, Kentucky beat LSU, Purdue routed Minnesota and Buffalo won at Toledo. But the market expected about 40 wins — it was only slightly off.

The model was way off

58 vs 75

Wins versus what its own probabilities expected. On the 57 games with a line it went 33–24 picking winners; simply taking every market favorite went 38–19.

Too many upset picks

17

Underdogs it picked to win outright, about twice its usual number — most at 50–64%. Six won: UCLA at Oregon, Iowa, Houston, USC, South Alabama and UNLV.

FCS games

24–22

FCS-vs-FCS games, where the model averaged 70% confidence. Those ratings lean on preseason recruiting and portal data, and this week they looked stale.
